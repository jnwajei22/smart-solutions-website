Compare the payout with all person-hours including travel, vehicle costs, materials, acquisition/platform fees and rework reserve.

A first job should fit available tools and transport, have a written scope and positive contribution, and have a confirmed crew and appointment. Do not use anticipated work to promise a date the partners have not accepted.
